# Exact venues and related locations

The production regression exposed two separate gaps: the recognized brand/aliases could displace the prominent full storefront sign before Google search, and the shortlist could only confirm one venue. Recognition now supports bounded `clue.signage` (150 characters), tied to the public venue clue. Vision must preserve the prominent primary sign, normalizing line breaks to spaces, and exclude private/incidental OCR. Search planning reserves its first existing slot for the strongest signage-backed identity, including after cited enrichment; arbitrary visibleText is never queried.

Candidate application metadata distinguishes `likely_exact`, `plausible_exact`, and `related_chain_location` (with `related_branch` reserved). With signage, exact/reordered/distinctive-equivalent sign evidence earns likely_exact; strong-partial/bounded-typo sign evidence earns plausible_exact. Only weak/absent photographed-sign identity plus independently supported possibleChain identity can receive the related label. Related candidates always rank below exact/plausible photographed-venue candidates. Without signage, the existing discrete meaningful identity classes continue to work. Generic-only names cannot authorize identity or chain membership. Weak query-relevant candidates may still be possible variants, without being promoted into chain members.

Related inference requires structured Recognition.possibleChain with meaningful identity, a bounded whole-token brand anchor (at most three added provider tokens), and compatible/related category. It is a discovery-time hypothesis, not an authoritative Google chain claim. ChainSchema/saveChain/getChain remain available but do not represent uncertain membership/provenance; this workflow therefore does not automatically write Chain records or chainId onto Places.

An attempt retains a transient provider scope across phases (including the production image-processing wrapper). At most two initial plus two enriched Text Search requests remain available. At most **one** additional related-location query is allowed, only with supported structured brand identity, a compatible eligible seed and a safe locality scope. A bound high-confidence linguistic intent allows a query in the user's normalized explicit city even when seed provider locality is unknown. The raw cityOverride and its aliases/country constraints remain intact; provider city never replaces it. Without accepted normalization, a matched seed locality is still required for explicit-city expansion. Without an explicit city, a reliable and unambiguous provider city is required. A verified exact-identity address contradiction must not be bypassed via a weaker Recognition match when selecting a chain seed. Generic category words are not structured chain evidence. It searches that brand's locations in the known city, enforcing city and country again. There is no group-wide discovery loop. Each response processes ten rows; slots are bounded at fifty. Related results share the same ID deduplication map. Different IDs remain separate branches. The display/persistence selection bound is **eight** candidates.

## City intent and display

A city correction makes one separate bounded linguistic normalization call (20 seconds, no tools, no web search, no venue input). Only high-confidence aliases bound to that exact accepted city are used. Venue verification success/citations are not required. Undefined, invalid/low-confidence output or an exception makes this auxiliary call unavailable; Google still searches with the raw cityOverride and no linguistic aliases. A fixed locality_normalization outcome records this without city/model content: ok means accepted high-confidence intent; unavailable covers undefined/low confidence and auxiliary-call exceptions; invalid means malformed intent or an incorrect input binding. Production low-confidence output already becomes undefined, and explicitly returned low confidence now has the same unavailable diagnostic. Model uncertainty does not invent geography. Cross-script provider/user text without common comparable aliases remains unknown; known comparable city/country or street-number contradictions still exclude results. No city or transliteration lookup table is added.

Provider city display is independent of semantic intent comparison. Reliable locality/postal components may be rendered as “Город по данным Google” even when user/provider equivalence is unknown. Matched typed administrative aliases can also corroborate intent, including punctuation/spacing/diacritic equivalents. Unmatched province/region components remain display-only providerContext (labelled as region, not confirmed city). A formattedAddress segment alone never establishes verified city identity or supplies a provider-city expansion scope. Place Details refresh requests addressComponents for that purpose. Provider city/content remains transient.

## Telegram and persistence

One eligible candidate produces a singular explicit-confirmation card, including a low-confidence related/weak result. Legacy pending one-item shortlists are upgraded to that same state with a new revision.

Two to eight candidates produce one coherent menu with transient names, city/address, Maps links, Google attribution and exact/possible/related labels. Checkboxes, Select all and Clear selection only update `selectedCandidateIndices`; they retain every candidate and create no Place. Each update increments the discovery revision, replaces opaque callback tokens and calls editMessageReplyMarkup on the same menu; compact numeric buttons need no transient provider names. Toggle/all/clear and final confirmation perform zero Place Details requests. Old/sibling/replayed callbacks are fenced by message, revision, expiry and leases. An interrupted keyboard edit resumes the applied selection rather than toggling twice. Google content is refreshed by ID after lost transient memory; it is never copied into interaction documents. One render sends at most **four** Telegram messages: up to **three** numbered continuations plus one control message. Short sets that fit the 3800 UTF-16 text budget use one message. Each candidate has bounded name/city/address previews and at most three third-party credit previews (160 UTF-16 units each); excessive credits carry an explicit shortened-attribution notice and a Google Maps link. Google Maps attribution is always present, safe source URIs remain clickable, and no candidate is discarded to fit credits. Links use Telegram text_link entities, preserving full Maps/source URLs outside the text budget and UTF-16 entity offsets. A continuation holds at most three candidate cards; the domain bound is eight, so oversized schema-valid content cannot cause an arbitrary sendMessage burst. Controls on the final message cover the complete selected set. Retry markers and tokens retain only bounded continuation message IDs, never provider text; already sent continuations are skipped on retry. Long provider content cannot throw telegram_shortlist_too_large. City change, cancel and successful confirmation best-effort delete up to three original continuations, carrying their IDs across checkbox revisions. If deletion fails, a fixed inactive-list edit is attempted. Both cleanup and metadata-read failure are swallowed; cleanup cannot roll back committed Places or poison callback retries.

“Добавить выбранные (N)” is the final confirmation action. One bounded Firestore transaction reads all selected Place identities first, validates all records, creates only absent Places, then marks the discovery confirmed. Any failure rolls back the entire batch. Repeats reuse deterministic canonicalPlaceId identities and cannot create duplicates; existing user labels are preserved.

`confirmedPlaceIds` records all confirmed IDs. Optional legacy `confirmedPlaceId` continues to hold the first confirmed ID for old consumers; old single-place documents parse without migration. Failed Discoveries cannot carry either confirmation field. Confirmed ID lists must be unique and their first ID must agree with the legacy field; selection indices must be unique and refer to present candidates. Terminal confirmation/cancellation ignores stale selection callbacks. New multi-confirmations retain the full candidate list plus selection indices, allowing each confirmed identity to be associated with its independent evidence. Backfill reads all confirmed IDs, matches provider identity, and never copies the photographed branch label to related locations. Projection still operates on independent canonical Place documents and refreshes Google content by ID; its architecture is unchanged.

Durable Google Places retain only identity, permitted references and independently sourced application labels. Google display names, addresses, coordinates, types and attribution content are absent from Place/Discovery/interaction documents. Related relationship/confidence enums are application metadata on Discovery, not permanent provider content or verified chain membership.

## Optional web enrichment failures

If Google firstPass already produced alternatives or a city_unknown prompt, a thrown SearchProvider.verify call or its validated unavailable result preserves that original human-reviewable result. No extra candidates are fabricated and its evidence is retained. A fixed optional_web_enrichment diagnostic records degraded plus verification_failed/verification_unavailable and alternatives/city_unknown only. Successful web enrichment continues through the existing Google phase and conflict checks. This recovery boundary does not include VerificationSchema parsing, Google calls, or POI adaptation. When no human-reviewable first result exists, required verification failures keep their previous failure/retry behavior.

## Chain expansion diagnostics and cityless planning

Vision explicitly assesses possibleChain for every clue, retaining full signage separately and omitting unsupported family claims. This remains optional model evidence; it cannot be inferred from a previously saved Place or assumed present because recognition succeeded. place_search_plan emits signagePresent, possibleChainPresent and unscopedQueryPlanned booleans only. With no explicit/verified city and a weak Vision/workspace area hint, the second existing ordinary query slot tries the full primary structured identity without that hint. No extra ordinary request or raw OCR/query logging is introduced.

google_related_expansion emits no_chain_evidence, no_eligible_seed, provider_city_unavailable, locality_unknown, conflicting_locality or expanded; scope is none/explicit_normalized/explicit_matched/provider. localityEvidence distinguishes structured components, display-only address_context and absent evidence. Presence flags never contain the underlying strings. Five Text Search requests per attempt and one related lookup remain the maximum; shortlist, atomic confirmation, retry fencing, licensing and export architecture are unchanged.

The production summary cannot establish the actual addressComponents shape, possibleChain presence, or exact reason Google returned zero cityless rows. Confirmed implementation constraints were the matched-locality/provider-city requirement for expansion, narrow provider-city extraction, normalized-exact component comparison, and area hints present in every hinted cityless query. Linguistic normalization success does not imply provider locality verification. New diagnostics distinguish missing evidence from those constraints without storing/logging provider or user text.
