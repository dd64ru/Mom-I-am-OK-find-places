# Google Places primary resolver

The production composition is `GooglePlacesPoi → FallbackPoi → NominatimPoi`. OpenAI vision/web verification supply bounded text only. Coordinates come from deterministic providers and require explicit Telegram confirmation. Google display/location content remains transient; the confirmed durable record retains only Google identity and evidence references.

## Protocol and matching

Use [Places API (New) Text Search](https://developers.google.com/maps/documentation/places/web-service/reference/rest/v1/places/searchText): `POST https://places.googleapis.com/v1/places:searchText`. Google runs first on vision clues: at most two deduplicated queries, followed only when needed by one OpenAI enrichment invocation and at most two additional Google queries (hard maximum four per resolution attempt), a requested pageSize of ten and a ten-second deadline per query, no redirects and an 8 MiB streamed response bound. Only structured venue/native names, canonical/corrected locality, address clue and country are queried; images, visible text, Telegram captions/conversation/IDs are excluded. English is preferred; native names and cited aliases remain useful.

The field mask requests only `places.id`, `places.displayName`, `places.formattedAddress`, `places.location`, `places.types`, `places.addressComponents`, `places.attributions` and `nextPageToken`. Display name and geographic validation require Text Search Pro fields; no reviews, photos, opening hours, ratings or contact details are requested. A later ID-based refresh uses a separate bounded Place Details read, described below. There is no wildcard, pagination/research loop or Google response cache.

Names use bounded deterministic scoring, never Google ranking alone: normalized exact match 0.96, token-multiset equality/reordering 0.94, one-edit typo in Latin tokens of 7–64 characters 0.86, and distinctive partial signage 0.72. Generic restaurant/cafe/hotel/store/university words alone do not authorize a match. Partial matching needs a distinctive token of at least seven characters and at most one additional distinctive token; unrelated words are rejected. Multiple short distinctive tokens can identify a full name such as Sun Yat-Sen University. Scores are capped at 1 and receive locality +0.08, known matching country +0.04, compatible category +0.05, related category +0.03, unknown/new category +0, and clearly unrelated known category −0.30 and matching address +0.06. A result requires score >=0.88 and margin >=0.12 over the runner-up. Similar branches remain ambiguous; lower-than-threshold competing candidates still participate in the margin. This deterministic confidence is an application heuristic, not a calibrated probability.

Explicit incompatible country or canonical city and conflicting comparable house numbers reject. Missing country/city/address/types are neutral, not contradictions; only explicit matching geography earns its bonus. Locality is stronger evidence than postal town/district; one matching canonical locality can coexist with a different layer. Without a locality, matching postal/admin aliases can add evidence, but unfamiliar hierarchy is neutral. For Chinese municipal/city components, an administrative name ending in 市 is explicit city evidence, without a city-name lookup table. Country components need not be unique; missing/uninterpretable country metadata is neutral. Known type is a scoring signal, never an eligibility whitelist: cafe/restaurant are related; university versus cafe or museum incurs a strong penalty. Unknown types use the verified/vision category for transient UI. Address is positive evidence, not a requirement. Only explicit comparable numbers on both sides permit a hard number-conflict rejection; ranges, building/subpremise notation and omitted numbers remain neutral. Street-only differences do not introduce fuzzy branch selection. House-number prefixes `No.`, `No` and `№` normalize away; Rd/Road, St/Street, Ave/Avenue, Blvd/Boulevard and Ln/Lane stay equivalent. 158 never becomes 1580/159. There is no unrestricted edit distance, fuzzy address search or guessed coordinate offset.

Queries use bounded native/canonical names, aliases or reordered terms and a distinctive partial/alternative visual clue, plus canonical locality/address/country. They use structured evidence only, never visibleText/images/raw conversation. One strong result stops further requests; otherwise candidates are combined by provider ID within each phase of at most two queries, requesting at most forty result rows across both phases (provider deviations are bounded by transport size rather than rejected by an invented array cap). Conflicting coordinates for the same ID fail closed. Multiple visual/verified identities in one locality can compete; conflicting localities still require clarification. A page token with plausible results preserves ambiguity because unseen competitors cannot be excluded.

City override is semantic intent, not a byte-exact name. OpenAI verification may emit a separate bounded `localityIntent` (canonicalName, aliases, countryCode, confidence) even when it finds no venue citation. The client binds it to the exact accepted city reply; confidence below 0.9 or a stale binding is ignored. This permits spelling/case/transliteration normalization without a Shanghai table. It supplies no coordinates/ID and is never persisted as Google content. AI locality is enrichment evidence, not a pre-provider veto; explicit canonical Google locality/country must not contradict intent; missing or unclear hierarchy is neutral. Workspace hints remain weak. Model linguistic normalization is uncertain and must be judged against provider evidence.

Exactly one sufficiently separated ID is resolved. Similar scored candidates or a truncated matching page remain ambiguous. A contradictory duplicate ID/coordinate response is invalid. Google's [Place location / LatLng contract](https://developers.google.com/maps/documentation/places/web-service/reference/rest/v1/places#LatLng) specifies WGS84 unless otherwise stated; this implementation uses those coordinates directly without China-specific offsets or undocumented conversions.

## ADC, quota and owner preparation

Authentication uses [Application Default Credentials](https://cloud.google.com/docs/authentication/application-default-credentials) through `google-auth-library`, with the two operation-specific scopes `https://www.googleapis.com/auth/maps-platform.places.textsearch` and `https://www.googleapis.com/auth/maps-platform.places.details` (listed in the official Text Search and [Place Details contracts](https://developers.google.com/maps/documentation/places/web-service/reference/rest/v1/places/get)). Production uses the attached `places-runtime@mom-im-ok-places.iam.gserviceaccount.com` identity and short-lived tokens. Headers are `Authorization: Bearer ...`, `X-Goog-User-Project`, `X-Goog-FieldMask` and `Content-Type`. The library refreshes tokens; no long-lived token is persisted by this provider. Tokens are never put in URLs or logs. No API key, new Secret Manager secret, service-account JSON key, GitHub secret/variable or owner credential import is required.

Owner preparation after review: update the Cloud Shell checkout, run the metadata-only migration plan, review `placesApiConsumerAction`, then owner-run apply to enable `places.googleapis.com` and create/reuse `projects/mom-im-ok-places/roles/placesApiConsumer`. Its only permission is `serviceusage.services.use`, bound to the runtime account on that quota project. See [quota project permissions](https://cloud.google.com/docs/quotas/set-quota-project). The role definition and unconditional runtime binding are validated fail-closed; extra/deleted permissions or unexpected runtime grants require review. Existing datastore access, deploy/build/App Engine IAM, WIF and runtime secret grants are preserved. CI gets no service-enablement permission. Review billing/Places quotas before manually deploying the reviewed commit. The development task does not perform any of these live steps.

## Fallback, provenance and Telegram

No usable Google match, locality mismatch or ambiguity may try Nominatim. A resolved OSM candidate is accepted; Google ambiguity is preserved if OSM cannot resolve it, rather than choosing an arbitrary first Google result or pretending the city is unknown. Missing locality, insufficient evidence and explicit-city conflicts retain existing fail-closed states.

Only classified request timeout/abort, HTTP 429 and HTTP 5xx allow exception fallback, with fixed `google_places_fallback_used` diagnostic. ADC/config failures, HTTP 401/403, other request errors, malformed/oversized bodies and programming/contract errors fail closed through fixed diagnostics. Typed deterministic JSON/envelope/adaptation failures atomically mark Discovery `failed` with a fixed failureReason and no candidates; Ingress marks `done` with the same reason, emits one `provider_terminal_failure` JSON event on the winning revision, and the webhook acknowledges 200. Immediate retries do not repeat vision, web verification or POI calls. The same terminal handling covers ID refresh and strict internal POI adaptation (`poi_adaptation_failed`). Auth/config/request errors are not reclassified as successful/no-match or silently sent to OSM; transient timeout/429/5xx retains existing fallback/retry behavior. Upstream error bodies are never read/logged. Nominatim retains its own global rate gate, cache, User-Agent and OSM attribution; busy/rate/provider failures use the existing webhook retry path.

## Durable identity versus transient display

Google identity is `provider: google-places, id: <Place ID>`. The dedicated GooglePlaceId schema is a nonempty opaque string, with no charset or length guesses; internal Firestore document IdSchema remains bounded. Refresh encodes the ID as one path segment and links encode it as a query value. Canonical IDs continue to hash that identity, so repeated submissions dedupe transactionally while distinct branches remain distinct. A Google `Place` stores only id/workspaceId, providerIdentity, source/evidence references, confirmed/archived status, user tags and timestamps. It has no canonicalName/nativeName/aliases/category/confidence, formatted address, coordinates, Google types or attribution metadata. Independent vision Recognition remains in Discovery, explicitly separate from provider authority; web-verification citation references remain evidence. The provider's displayName is never silently relabeled as independently sourced.

The strict durable schemas also prevent Google content from being retained in pending, cancelled or confirmed Discovery candidates: `StoredCandidate` holds the Google identity, deterministic resolution marker and references only. The service passes a separate in-memory `DiscoveryView.liveCandidate` directly to Telegram for the initial proposal; this field is not part of the Firestore Discovery schema. Retrying a proposal after reloading its Discovery refreshes the live display by ID without rerunning vision/web search. No Google content cache, TTL cleanup dependency or persisted display snapshot is introduced. Raw live Google candidates and enriched Google Places are rejected on every repository write path.

Telegram proposals continue to show live name/address, a Google Maps link and required Google/supplied third-party attribution. Google content is displayed as a plain Russian message rather than on Telegram's non-Google venue map. That message remains with Telegram; this separation prevents it becoming our reusable Firestore dataset and does not claim deletion/control of already-delivered external messages. OSM proposals retain venue rendering and OSM attribution.

`GooglePlacesPoi.refresh(providerIdentity)` (also available through `FallbackPoi`/the optional `PoiProvider.refresh` port) returns a transient `PlaceDisplay`, never a canonical `Place`. It makes one authenticated `GET https://places.googleapis.com/v1/places/<Place ID>` with field mask `id,displayName,formattedAddress,location,attributions`, a ten-second deadline and 8 MiB response bound. IDs and returned identity are checked, including missing/invalid location/display fields. It uses the same ADC/quota headers, fixed safe error codes and no upstream-error-body logging as Text Search. Refresh errors remain errors; a stored Google identity cannot silently become an OSM point. Present attributions must accompany any later display.

Future projection/export adapters must request a fresh provider view by the stored Google ID and independently respect [Google Places policies](https://developers.google.com/maps/documentation/places/web-service/policies) for the destination, attribution and retention. Refresh availability does not grant unrestricted caching or external redistribution. No external export adapter is implemented here. For Google records, absence of canonical coordinates is intentional; the Place ID is the durable anchor exempt from the provider-content caching restriction. Nominatim Places retain their previous canonical name/address/WGS84 coordinates/category/evidence/attribution handling under OSM/ODbL.

This fix precedes production deployment of the Google resolver. It does not migrate or scrub live cloud data, deploy, change the webhook or make live Places calls. If Google content from an earlier version exists elsewhere, it requires separate owner-reviewed cleanup; these schemas reject the old Google-enriched records rather than trust their fields.

## Operational telemetry

Runtime logs JSON events with fixed fields only. `place_pipeline_stage` contains `stage`, bounded integer `durationMs` and `status`: stages are image_download, vision, web_verification, google_places and nominatim. Status values are ok, error, resolved, no_match, ambiguous, unresolved or city_unknown. `place_resolution` contains provider (google-places/nominatim/none), result (resolved/unresolved/city_unknown/failed) and the composed POI resolution duration. Download/inference/search stages are separately measured, including city correction/retry search. No venue/city/address/coordinates, images, raw updates, responses, credentials or user identifiers are logged. Error diagnostics remain English fixed codes; Telegram UI is Russian.

`google_places_filter` is emitted once per actual Text Search query. It contains only query number (1–2) and phase (`google_first_pass`/`google_enriched_pass`), integer counts bounded by the transport response: returned, complete, nameStrong, categoryCompatible, localityCompatible, addressCompatible, accepted; result enum resolved/ambiguous/no_match; and fixed rejection counts no_name_match, category_conflict, country_conflict, locality_conflict, address_conflict, below_threshold, ambiguous_score. No names, text, addresses, coordinates, IDs, scores, tokens or upstream bodies are included. Diagnostics are best effort and do not change resolution.

## Reviewed external contract and adapter

Reviewed current official contracts on 2026-10-05: [SearchTextResponse](https://developers.google.com/maps/documentation/places/web-service/reference/rest/v1/places/searchText), [Place, LocalizedText, AddressComponent, Attribution and LatLng](https://developers.google.com/maps/documentation/places/web-service/reference/rest/v1/places), [Place IDs](https://developers.google.com/maps/documentation/places/web-service/place-id), [place types](https://developers.google.com/maps/documentation/places/web-service/place-types), and [field masks](https://developers.google.com/maps/documentation/places/web-service/choose-fields). Google explicitly gives Place IDs no maximum length, allows empty component types, and warns that component count/order/types/presence change. Attribution providerUri is a URI, not a mandatory HTTPS URL. None of the consumed text/array fields documents the old guessed maxima (300/1000 characters, 30/10 components/types, 10 attributions, 3000-character page tokens). A mask selects fields; it does not guarantee every optional field exists. Types evolve.

`google-places-contract.ts` interprets the search envelope and adapts each row independently into a provider DTO. External objects ignore/strip unknown fields, including nested fields. Required consumed identity/name/location are checked; optional address/types/components/attributions can be absent. Coordinates still require finite numeric WGS84 lat/lng within bounds. Unusable rows are skipped; usable siblings remain candidates. DTOs then adapt explicitly into strict transient Candidate/PlaceDisplay schemas, and durable Google identity-only schemas retain their strict invariants. No raw provider JSON is persisted. Unknown fields never become application properties. Consumed credential echoes are skipped before rendering/persistence.

The 8 MiB transport ceiling bounds streamed decoded bytes before JSON parsing and is intentionally generous for ten requested places with the narrow mask, including unusually long text/IDs/attributions. It is an application memory/abuse bound, not a Google field-length promise. Exceeding it yields `google_places_response_too_large`; invalid JSON yields `google_places_invalid_json`; uninterpretable envelope yields `google_places_top_level_invalid`. Malformed rows do not fail the whole search. Internal impossible adaptation and contradictory duplicate ID/coordinates remain deterministic failures, not fallback authorization.

`google_places_parse` records only topLevel (`ok`, `invalid_json`, `top_level_invalid`, `response_too_large`), rowsReturned/rowsUsable/rowsSkipped and counts for missing_id/missing_display_name/missing_location/invalid_location/unsupported_shape. A missing address is usable, so is not a skip. Matching logs remain count-only. No schema issue payloads, upstream bodies, IDs, names, locations, attribution text/URIs, queries, Telegram content or credentials are logged.

Attribution URI policy belongs to Telegram rendering: safe HTTP/HTTPS without URL credentials may be shown as links; other/malformed/overlong URIs render provider text only. UI text preview limits do not reject external DTOs. Provider attribution text has control characters removed. The existing Google-content persistence/licensing boundary, ADC/scopes/quota project, owner API enablement/IAM, Nominatim gate/cache and canonical hash IDs are unchanged.

## Search eligibility and resolution control flow

`google-search-plan.ts` accepts every schema-valid clue regardless of confidence, limits input to three clues, and builds at most two deduplicated bounded structured queries from native/canonical names, aliases, reordered/distinctive terms and optional hints. Neither city nor country is required. With neither, queries omit locality and regionCode. Vision/workspace area hints inform queries but do not impose a hard provider-city veto. Explicit cityOverride (including its bound enriched semantic aliases) is a strong provider constraint; country evidence and comparable house numbers still reject contradictions after Google returns. AI confidence ranks inputs and is counted in diagnostics, not used as an eligibility threshold. Final name score >=0.88 and runner margin >=0.12 are unchanged; no first-ranked Google result is accepted on ranking alone.

`DiscoveryService` uses the optional POI firstPass port, implemented by GooglePlacesPoi/FallbackPoi in production. One confident first-pass result skips OpenAI web verification and ordinary no-match OSM fallback. An unresolved/ambiguous result invokes OpenAiSearch once (at most two web_search operations, enforced by existing streaming evidence validation), then one two-query enriched Google phase. The enriched phase retains the existing bounded Nominatim fallback for no-match/unusable/ambiguous results and classified transient Google outages. No other error authorizes exception fallback. There is no research/pagination loop. No clues means no Google I/O or web enrichment. Legacy non-Google POI adapters without firstPass retain their existing verification port behavior.

Without city intent, strongly comparable candidates in different or unclear provider localities, or plausible truncated unknown-city results, ask city. One strong separated candidate can resolve from Google identity/name/location even without prior AI locality. Same-city branch ambiguity remains unresolved: asking the same city automatically would not help. Correction reuses Recognition and runs the same bounded pipeline with explicit semantic locality. A prior city ambiguity is preserved if enrichment/fallback only yields no_match/insufficient_evidence. Each new accepted correction is its own resolution attempt; transient webhook retries can repeat bounded work, while deterministic failure states retain the previous no-retry guarantees.

`place_search_plan` reports only phase, visionClues (0–3), confidenceHigh (>=0.85), confidenceMedium (>=0.5 and <0.85), confidenceLow, localityKnown and queriesPlanned (0–2). These confidence bands are diagnostic counts, never gates. `google_places` pipeline events are emitted around actual HTTP requests, including request errors; a zero-query plan emits no misleading Google I/O stage. Phase enums are google_first_pass, web_enrichment, google_enriched_pass. Web enrichment retains the web_verification stage with its phase; parse/filter events remain count-only. No clue/query/venue/city/address/coordinates/ID/user text or credentials enter logs.
