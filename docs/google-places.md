# Google Places primary resolver

The production composition is `GooglePlacesPoi → FallbackPoi → NominatimPoi`. OpenAI vision/web verification supply bounded text only. Coordinates come from deterministic providers and require explicit Telegram confirmation. Google display/location content remains transient; the confirmed durable record retains only Google identity and evidence references.

## Protocol and matching

Use [Places API (New) Text Search](https://developers.google.com/maps/documentation/places/web-service/reference/rest/v1/places/searchText): `POST https://places.googleapis.com/v1/places:searchText`. For single-venue inputs, Google runs first on vision clues: at most two deduplicated queries, followed only when needed by one OpenAI enrichment invocation and at most two additional Google queries (four ordinary requests plus at most one bounded related-location request per resolution attempt), a requested pageSize of ten and a ten-second deadline per query, no redirects and an 8 MiB streamed response bound. Only structured venue/native names, canonical/corrected locality, address clue and country are queried; images, visible text, Telegram captions/conversation/IDs are excluded. English is preferred; native names and cited aliases remain useful.

Recommendation lists use separate explicit brand selection: up to eight identities, up to five selected brands, one independently matched full-brand query each in the user’s city, and a fair eight-Place shortlist. No additional list web/enriched/automatic-chain pass is added. A single-venue user can request potentially related locations without optional model chain evidence. See [recommendation workflows](recommendation-lists.md) for budgets, short-name exact matching, sibling-failure handling and diagnostics.

The field mask requests only `places.id`, `places.displayName`, `places.formattedAddress`, `places.location`, `places.types`, `places.addressComponents`, `places.attributions` and `nextPageToken`. Display name and geographic validation require Text Search Pro fields; no reviews, photos, opening hours, ratings or contact details are requested. A later ID-based refresh uses a separate bounded Place Details read, described below. There is no wildcard, pagination/research loop or Google response cache.

Names produce explicit evidence: `exact` (normalized full name), `reordered` (same full token multiset), `distinctive_equivalent` (same distinctive token multiset, ignoring generic descriptors), `strong_partial`, `bounded_typo`, `weak` or `none`. Generic restaurant/cafe/hotel/store/university words alone never authorize identity. Meaningful identity requires a distinctive token of at least four characters or Han text, or at least two distinctive tokens totaling seven characters. Partial signage requires a distinctive token of at least seven characters and exactly one additional distinctive token in the returned name. Typo tolerance is at most one edit per distinctive Latin token of 7–64 characters, with equal distinctive token counts. No unrestricted fuzzy matching is introduced. “Alimentari” versus “Alimentari Grande” is distinctive-equivalent; generic “Grande” cannot cause a raw-token-count penalty. “Grande Alimentari” versus “Alimentari Grande” is reordered.

`google-candidate-decision.ts` separates eligibility, ranking, confidence and hard contradictions. Unique exact/reordered/distinctive-equivalent and meaningful strong_partial/bounded_typo identities may be surfaced with a usable deterministic Google ID/location and no hard geographic/comparable-number conflict or unresolved competition. Unknown locality is neutral even with explicit cityOverride; it lowers confidence rather than discarding a plausible candidate. The override still constrains search and improves ranking when corroborated. Country/category evidence never counts as city corroboration. Generic-only/unrelated identities never qualify. A meaningful weak overlap with locality/address/web/category support may qualify at low confidence; one eligible candidate uses singular confirmation. Supported chain identity is labelled as an inferred related location, distinct from exact evidence. Unrelated names remain excluded. Numeric ranks order candidates; they are not absolute acceptance gates.

Confidence is deterministic application metadata, separate from AI clue confidence: strong identity with matching locality is **high**, strong identity with unknown locality is **medium**. Partial/typo identity with matching locality/address or verified venue web evidence is **medium**; otherwise it is **low**, including related category or country alone. Multiple plausible candidates require human selection; ranking orders their display without selecting a branch. The Telegram card labels every Google proposal and asks the user to check its Google Maps link and confirm. Medium/low cards explicitly say “Нашёл возможный вариант. Но это не точно 🙂”. No candidate becomes a canonical Place before explicit confirmation. The confidence enum may persist with the discovery identity so retry cards retain the same wording; Google name/address/location/types remain transient under the existing licensing boundary. Legacy discoveries with no confidence enum render conservatively as low.

Ranking compares identity strength first (strong identities, partial/typo, weak, none), then locality/address corroboration strength, then a heuristic rank: exact .96, reordered .94, distinctive-equivalent .90, bounded typo .86, partial .72, weak up to .10. Add city .08, country .04, address .06, category compatible .05/related .03/unknown 0/conflict −.30; clamp to [0,1]. These values are ordering/diagnostic heuristics, not probabilities or acceptance cutoffs. `GOOGLE_MATCH_THRESHOLD` is removed. Category disagreement alone never vetoes strong identity or chooses between equivalent branches.

Multiple plausible IDs produce `alternatives` with at most **eight** candidates. Names are never used as deduplication keys: separate branches retain separate IDs. The resolver's transient `beginAttempt()` scope shares candidate and diagnostic-slot maps across up to two first-pass and two enriched queries, with no durable cache. Repeated IDs retain the best observed evidence; hard-conflicting rows are excluded. Pagination stays bounded: a next-page marker requests human selection from the available results, never fetching extra pages or claiming uniqueness. A sole meaningful, untruncated candidate keeps the existing direct high/medium/low proposal. A sole eligible weak/related candidate uses singular final confirmation.

Core stores alternative identities/evidence as Discovery `needs_selection`, plus transient `liveAlternatives` for initial rendering. Reloads use the existing Place Details refresh abstraction. Telegram sends one multi-select menu of up to eight entries, each with live Google name/city/address, its Maps link, attribution and low-confidence warning. “Выбрать вариант” uses a separate opaque token bound to the discovery revision and candidate index. Checkboxes retain the full candidate list and update selectedCandidateIndices with a new revision; they write no Place. The final “Добавить выбранные” action atomically confirms all selected identities. Competing selections, stale tokens, expiry and cancellations cannot select/save an obsolete candidate. Transient refresh retries resume the already selected revision without repeating search. Change city remains available from every card.

Explicit incompatible country or canonical city and conflicting comparable house numbers reject. Missing country/city/address/types are neutral, not contradictions; only explicit matching geography earns its bonus. Locality is stronger evidence than postal town/district; one matching canonical locality can coexist with a different layer. Without a locality, matching postal/admin aliases can add evidence, but unfamiliar hierarchy is neutral. Matching locality/postal/admin-level-1/admin-level-2 aliases corroborate city intent in any response language. English Shanghai/Beijing/Tianjin/Chongqing need no 市 suffix and no city lookup table. Nonmatching broader provinces remain neutral; explicit locality/postal city, Chinese admin-level-2 cities and admin-level-1 names marked 市 can establish conflicts. FormattedAddress tokens/segments are display-only address context, not verified city identity; reliable structured components are required for provider locality corroboration. Component comparisons allow separators/diacritics without fuzzy matching. Unmatched administrative regions may display as providerContext, never as a confirmed city. A typed incompatible city cannot be rescued by matching text elsewhere in the address. Country components need not be unique; missing/uninterpretable country metadata is neutral. Known type is a scoring signal, never an eligibility whitelist: cafe/restaurant are related; university versus cafe or museum incurs a strong penalty. Unknown types use the verified/vision category for transient UI. Address is positive evidence, not a requirement. Only explicit comparable numbers on both sides permit a hard number-conflict rejection; ranges, building/subpremise notation and omitted numbers remain neutral. Street-only differences do not introduce fuzzy branch selection. House-number prefixes `No.`, `No` and `№` normalize away; Rd/Road, St/Street, Ave/Avenue, Blvd/Boulevard and Ln/Lane stay equivalent. 158 never becomes 1580/159. There is no unrestricted edit distance, fuzzy address search or guessed coordinate offset.

Queries use bounded native/canonical names, aliases or reordered terms and a distinctive partial/alternative visual clue, plus canonical locality/address/country. They use structured evidence only, never visibleText/images/raw conversation. One strong result stops further requests; otherwise candidates are combined by provider ID within each phase of at most two queries, requesting at most forty result rows across both phases (provider deviations are bounded by transport size rather than rejected by an invented array cap). Conflicting coordinates for the same ID fail closed. Multiple visual/verified identities in one locality can compete; conflicting localities still require clarification. A page token with plausible results preserves ambiguity because unseen competitors cannot be excluded.

City override is semantic intent, not a byte-exact name. OpenAI verification may emit a separate bounded `localityIntent` (canonicalName, aliases, countryCode, confidence) even when it finds no venue citation. The client binds it to the exact accepted city reply; confidence below 0.9 or a stale binding is ignored. This permits spelling/case/transliteration normalization without a Shanghai table. It supplies no coordinates/ID and is never persisted as Google content. AI locality is enrichment evidence, not a pre-provider veto; explicit canonical Google locality/country must not contradict intent; missing or unclear hierarchy is neutral. Workspace hints remain weak. Model linguistic normalization is uncertain and must be judged against provider evidence.

Exactly one acceptable, sufficiently separated identity is resolved. Comparable competing identities or a truncated matching page remain ambiguous. A contradictory duplicate ID/coordinate response is invalid. Google's [Place location / LatLng contract](https://developers.google.com/maps/documentation/places/web-service/reference/rest/v1/places#LatLng) specifies WGS84 unless otherwise stated; the adapter keeps those coordinates exactly as returned. Inside mainland China they are aligned to Google's GCJ-02-offset map data; the map projection corrects such a point once, to its standard WGS84 position, before any export (see [map projection](map-projection.md)).

## ADC, quota and owner preparation

Authentication uses [Application Default Credentials](https://cloud.google.com/docs/authentication/application-default-credentials) through `google-auth-library`, with the two operation-specific scopes `https://www.googleapis.com/auth/maps-platform.places.textsearch` and `https://www.googleapis.com/auth/maps-platform.places.details` (listed in the official Text Search and [Place Details contracts](https://developers.google.com/maps/documentation/places/web-service/reference/rest/v1/places/get)). Production uses the attached `places-runtime@mom-im-ok-places.iam.gserviceaccount.com` identity and short-lived tokens. Headers are `Authorization: Bearer ...`, `X-Goog-User-Project`, `X-Goog-FieldMask` and `Content-Type`. The library refreshes tokens; no long-lived token is persisted by this provider. Tokens are never put in URLs or logs. No API key, new Secret Manager secret, service-account JSON key, GitHub secret/variable or owner credential import is required.

Owner preparation after review: update the Cloud Shell checkout, run the metadata-only migration plan, review `placesApiConsumerAction`, then owner-run apply to enable `places.googleapis.com` and create/reuse `projects/mom-im-ok-places/roles/placesApiConsumer`. Its only permission is `serviceusage.services.use`, bound to the runtime account on that quota project. See [quota project permissions](https://cloud.google.com/docs/quotas/set-quota-project). The role definition and unconditional runtime binding are validated fail-closed; extra/deleted permissions or unexpected runtime grants require review. Existing datastore access, deploy/build/App Engine IAM, WIF and runtime secret grants are preserved. CI gets no service-enablement permission. Review billing/Places quotas before manually deploying the reviewed commit. The development task does not perform any of these live steps.

## Fallback, provenance and Telegram

No usable Google match or locality mismatch may try Nominatim. Google alternatives return directly without an OSM automatic choice. A resolved OSM candidate is accepted; A Google shortlist cannot be silently replaced by a fallback choice. Unusable identity and explicit geographic/address conflicts retain fail-closed states; missing locality alone does not reject a unique plausible identity.

Only classified request timeout/abort, HTTP 429 and HTTP 5xx allow exception fallback, with fixed `google_places_fallback_used` diagnostic. ADC/config failures, HTTP 401/403, other request errors, malformed/oversized bodies and programming/contract errors fail closed through fixed diagnostics. Typed deterministic JSON/envelope/adaptation failures atomically mark Discovery `failed` with a fixed failureReason and no candidates; Ingress marks `done` with the same reason, emits one `provider_terminal_failure` JSON event on the winning revision, and the webhook acknowledges 200. Immediate retries do not repeat vision, web verification or POI calls. The same terminal handling covers ID refresh and strict internal POI adaptation (`poi_adaptation_failed`). Auth/config/request errors are not reclassified as successful/no-match or silently sent to OSM; transient timeout/429/5xx retains existing fallback/retry behavior. Upstream error bodies are never read/logged. Nominatim retains its own global rate gate, cache, User-Agent and OSM attribution; busy/rate/provider failures use the existing webhook retry path.

## Durable identity versus transient display

Google identity is `provider: google-places, id: <Place ID>`. The dedicated GooglePlaceId schema is a nonempty opaque string, with no charset or length guesses; internal Firestore document IdSchema remains bounded. Refresh encodes the ID as one path segment and links encode it as a query value. Canonical IDs continue to hash that identity, so repeated submissions dedupe transactionally while distinct branches remain distinct. A Google `Place` stores id/workspaceId, providerIdentity, source/evidence references, confirmed/archived status, user tags/timestamps and an optional application-owned label/labelSource pair derived only from independent Recognition or a future user label. Existing unlabeled records still parse. Google displayName never supplies that durable label. It has no canonicalName/nativeName/aliases/category/confidence, formatted address, coordinates, Google types or attribution metadata. Independent vision Recognition remains in Discovery, explicitly separate from provider authority; web-verification citation references remain evidence. The provider's displayName is never silently relabeled as independently sourced.

The strict durable schemas also prevent Google content from being retained in pending, cancelled or confirmed Discovery candidates: `StoredCandidate` holds the Google identity, deterministic resolution marker, references and optional recognitionClueIndex linking to independent Recognition; no provider display content is retained. The service passes a separate in-memory `DiscoveryView.liveCandidate` directly to Telegram for the initial proposal; this field is not part of the Firestore Discovery schema. Retrying a proposal after reloading its Discovery refreshes the live display by ID without rerunning vision/web search. No Google content cache, TTL cleanup dependency or persisted display snapshot is introduced. Raw live Google candidates and enriched Google Places are rejected on every repository write path.

Telegram proposals continue to show live name/address, a Google Maps link and required Google/supplied third-party attribution. Google content is displayed as a plain Russian message rather than on Telegram's non-Google venue map. That message remains with Telegram; this separation prevents it becoming our reusable Firestore dataset and does not claim deletion/control of already-delivered external messages. OSM proposals retain venue rendering and OSM attribution.

`GooglePlacesPoi.refresh(providerIdentity)` (also available through `FallbackPoi`/the optional `PoiProvider.refresh` port) returns a transient `PlaceDisplay`, never a canonical `Place`. It makes one authenticated `GET https://places.googleapis.com/v1/places/<Place ID>` with field mask `id,displayName,formattedAddress,location,addressComponents,attributions`, a ten-second deadline and 8 MiB response bound. IDs and returned identity are checked, including missing/invalid location/display fields. It uses the same ADC/quota headers, fixed safe error codes and no upstream-error-body logging as Text Search. Refresh errors remain errors; a stored Google identity cannot silently become an OSM point. Present attributions must accompany any later display.

Future projection/export adapters must request a fresh provider view by the stored Google ID and independently respect [Google Places policies](https://developers.google.com/maps/documentation/places/web-service/policies) for the destination, attribution and retention. Refresh availability does not grant unrestricted caching or external redistribution. The first read-only coordinate projection and GeoJSON/GPX/KML adapters are described in [map projection](map-projection.md); destination/retention permissions are not implied by refreshing coordinates. For Google records, absence of canonical coordinates is intentional; the Place ID is the durable anchor exempt from the provider-content caching restriction. Nominatim Places retain their previous canonical name/address/WGS84 coordinates/category/evidence/attribution handling under OSM/ODbL.

This fix precedes production deployment of the Google resolver. It does not migrate or scrub live cloud data, deploy, change the webhook or make live Places calls. If Google content from an earlier version exists elsewhere, it requires separate owner-reviewed cleanup; these schemas reject the old Google-enriched records rather than trust their fields.

## Operational telemetry

Runtime logs JSON events with fixed fields only. `place_pipeline_stage` contains `stage`, bounded integer `durationMs` and `status`: stages are image_download, vision, web_verification, google_places and nominatim. Status values are ok, error, resolved, no_match, ambiguous, unresolved or city_unknown. `place_resolution` contains provider (google-places/nominatim/none), result (resolved/unresolved/city_unknown/failed) and the composed POI resolution duration. Download/inference/search stages are separately measured, including city correction/retry search. No venue/city/address/coordinates, images, raw updates, responses, credentials or user identifiers are logged. Error diagnostics remain English fixed codes; Telegram UI is Russian.

`google_places_filter` is emitted once per actual Text Search query. It contains phase, query number and aggregate counts: returned, complete, nameStrong, categoryCompatible, cityCompatible, countryCompatible, addressCompatible, accepted; result resolved/alternatives/ambiguous/no_match; and fixed counts no_name_match, category_conflict, country_conflict, locality_conflict, address_conflict, insufficient_identity, ambiguous_competition. The retained category_conflict count records soft disagreement despite its legacy position under `rejected`; it is not a veto. `accepted` counts individually eligible rows before competition. Obsolete below_threshold/ambiguous_score counters are removed.

`google_places_decision` reports each evaluated query's top candidate (or top hard-rejected row when none survives): phase/query, nameEvidence, nameRankPermille, localityState, countryState, addressState, categoryState, finalRankPermille, runnerUpRankPermille, decision, candidateConfidence (high/medium/low for surfaced candidates; absent otherwise). State/decision/name values are fixed enums; ranks are bounded integers 0–1000. Decisions identify strong acceptance, partial acceptance with locality/address/web/category or no corroboration, hard contradiction, ambiguous competition or insufficient identity; shortlist_required means explicit selection is needed. No names, query/city text, addresses, coordinates, Place IDs, user content, tokens or raw provider bodies enter these events. Diagnostics are best effort.

Russian unresolved UX distinguishes “Не удалось найти подходящее место.”, “Нашлось несколько похожих мест. Уточни город.” and “Не удалось уверенно определить конкретное место.” Branch ambiguity does not ask for a better screenshot or expose scoring internals.

## Reviewed external contract and adapter

Reviewed current official contracts on 2026-10-05: [SearchTextResponse](https://developers.google.com/maps/documentation/places/web-service/reference/rest/v1/places/searchText), [Place, LocalizedText, AddressComponent, Attribution and LatLng](https://developers.google.com/maps/documentation/places/web-service/reference/rest/v1/places), [Place IDs](https://developers.google.com/maps/documentation/places/web-service/place-id), [place types](https://developers.google.com/maps/documentation/places/web-service/place-types), and [field masks](https://developers.google.com/maps/documentation/places/web-service/choose-fields). Google explicitly gives Place IDs no maximum length, allows empty component types, and warns that component count/order/types/presence change. Attribution providerUri is a URI, not a mandatory HTTPS URL. None of the consumed text/array fields documents the old guessed maxima (300/1000 characters, 30/10 components/types, 10 attributions, 3000-character page tokens). A mask selects fields; it does not guarantee every optional field exists. Types evolve.

`google-places-contract.ts` interprets the search envelope and adapts each row independently into a provider DTO. External objects ignore/strip unknown fields, including nested fields. Required consumed identity/name/location are checked; optional address/types/components/attributions can be absent. Coordinates still require finite numeric WGS84 lat/lng within bounds. Unusable rows are skipped; usable siblings remain candidates. DTOs then adapt explicitly into strict transient Candidate/PlaceDisplay schemas, and durable Google identity-only schemas retain their strict invariants. No raw provider JSON is persisted. Unknown fields never become application properties. Consumed credential echoes are skipped before rendering/persistence.

The 8 MiB transport ceiling bounds streamed decoded bytes before JSON parsing and is intentionally generous for ten requested places with the narrow mask, including unusually long text/IDs/attributions. It is an application memory/abuse bound, not a Google field-length promise. Exceeding it yields `google_places_response_too_large`; invalid JSON yields `google_places_invalid_json`; uninterpretable envelope yields `google_places_top_level_invalid`. Malformed rows do not fail the whole search. Internal impossible adaptation and contradictory duplicate ID/coordinates remain deterministic failures, not fallback authorization.

`google_places_parse` records only topLevel (`ok`, `invalid_json`, `top_level_invalid`, `response_too_large`), rowsReturned/rowsUsable/rowsSkipped and counts for missing_id/missing_display_name/missing_location/invalid_location/unsupported_shape. A missing address is usable, so is not a skip. Matching logs contain fixed evidence enums and bounded counts/ranks only. No schema issue payloads, upstream bodies, IDs, names, locations, attribution text/URIs, queries, Telegram content or credentials are logged.

Attribution URI policy belongs to Telegram rendering: safe HTTP/HTTPS without URL credentials may be shown as links; other/malformed/overlong URIs render provider text only. UI text preview limits do not reject external DTOs. Provider attribution text has control characters removed. The existing Google-content persistence/licensing boundary, ADC/scopes/quota project, owner API enablement/IAM, Nominatim gate/cache and canonical hash IDs are unchanged.

## Search eligibility and resolution control flow

`google-search-plan.ts` accepts every schema-valid clue regardless of confidence, limits input to three clues, and builds at most two deduplicated bounded structured queries from native/canonical names, aliases, reordered/distinctive terms and optional hints. Neither city nor country is required. With neither, queries omit locality and regionCode. Vision/workspace area hints inform queries but do not impose a hard provider-city veto. Explicit cityOverride (including its bound enriched semantic aliases) is a strong provider constraint; country evidence and comparable house numbers still reject contradictions after Google returns. AI confidence ranks inputs and is counted in diagnostics, not used as an eligibility threshold. Acceptance uses the evidence rules above; no first-ranked Google result is accepted on ranking alone.

`DiscoveryService` uses optional `beginAttempt` and `firstPass` ports, implemented by GooglePlacesPoi/FallbackPoi in production. One meaningful first-pass result skips OpenAI web verification and ordinary OSM fallback. Other results invoke OpenAiSearch once (at most two web_search operations), then one two-query enriched Google phase. If the optional web call throws or reports unavailable after a first-pass shortlist/city prompt exists, DiscoveryService retains that original result with a fixed degraded-enrichment event. Provider/schema/adaptation failures and required verification without a usable first result retain existing failure semantics. Alternatives retain deduplicated first-pass evidence in this transient attempt; enrichment can add support or exclude hard conflicts. No-match/unusable results and classified transient Google outages retain bounded Nominatim fallback. Alternatives bypass ordinary OSM fallback. No other error authorizes exception fallback. No clues means no Google I/O or enrichment; legacy providers retain their existing port behavior.

Comparable candidates, including different-city branches with no explicit reliable contradiction, now produce a shortlist instead of hiding behind a city question/unresolved result. The user may select or explicitly edit city. Correction reuses Recognition and runs a fresh bounded attempt with fresh slot numbering. Non-Google missing/ambiguous locality flows retain their existing city prompt.

`place_search_plan` reports only phase, visionClues (0–3), confidenceHigh (>=0.85), confidenceMedium (>=0.5 and <0.85), confidenceLow, localityKnown and queriesPlanned (0–2). These confidence bands are diagnostic counts, never gates. `google_places` pipeline events are emitted around actual HTTP requests, including request errors; a zero-query plan emits no misleading Google I/O stage. Phase enums are google_first_pass, web_enrichment, google_enriched_pass. Web enrichment retains the web_verification stage with its phase; parse/filter events remain count-only and decision events add fixed evidence enums and integer ranks. No clue/query/venue/city/address/coordinates/ID/user text or credentials enter logs.

## Production locality and branch regression

The Alimentari failure combined strong_partial identity with unknown locality evidence, despite English administrative components or transient formattedAddress naming the intended city. Google requests languageCode=en; a 市-only administrative heuristic cannot govern positive corroboration. All relevant component aliases now match normalized-exactly regardless of script, while formatted-address segments remain display-only context when reliably typed city is absent. Explicit city conflicts still veto; a different broader province alone does not. cityCompatible and countryCompatible counters replace the combined localityCompatible counter, which previously credited country agreement as city agreement.

Provider names can carry bounded explicit branch metadata: trailing parentheses ending in Branch or a Han store/branch marker, or a separator-delimited Branch suffix. Only a strongly matching or meaningful partial base identity uses that qualifier removal; generic/weak bases cannot be rescued. Ordinary parentheses such as Museum (Hotel) remain significant. Every Google branch remains a separate identity and participates in ambiguity. Recognition category vocabulary deli/grocery/food store/supermarket now shares the corresponding Google food group; unknown categories remain neutral and category disagreements remain soft.

Regression fixtures use an actual partial-style provider name, unknown category, no address clue/citation, Russian city input bound to semantic Shanghai/CN, English admin-level-1 Shanghai / Chinese 上海市 / formatted-address-only corroboration. They accept as accepted_partial_with_locality in one Google query. The workflow fixture with available canonical city intent skips web enrichment and shows Add. Translating an untranslated city reply into semantic aliases can still require the existing bounded enrichment; this fix does not invent transliteration equality or a city table. Once that intent and provider corroboration exist, no extra web citation/category/address checkbox is required. GOOGLE_MATCH_THRESHOLD remains removed.

## Candidate topology diagnostics

`google_places_candidate` emits per usable provider row, after assigning a stable slot by ID within the transient attempt. Slots span first/enriched phases and are bounded by fifty processed rows (four ordinary queries plus one related-location query of ten). If a response exceeds the requested ten usable rows, parse diagnostics still count all rows; processing stays at ten and treats the result as truncated, requiring selection; repeated IDs keep the same slot. Fields are only phase, query (1–2), candidateSlot, providerRank (1–10), nameEvidence, nameRankPermille, localityState, countryState, addressState, categoryState, finalRankPermille, candidateConfidence, decision and seenInMultipleQueries. The repetition boolean counts distinct phase/query pairs, not duplicates within one response. Row decisions describe eligibility (`eligible_weak_alternative` for supported weak overlap), hard rejection or insufficient identity. The query-level decision reports `shortlist_required` with low confidence when selection is necessary; pipeline resolution reports alternatives. Row confidence describes individual evidence strength, while shortlist UI is low because branch choice is unverified. No provider IDs, names, queries, addresses, coordinates, user content, tokens or raw response dumps are logged or stored for debugging.
